Pork Ribs Sinigang

Description

Pork Ribs Sinigang is a Sinigang variation made with pork ribs, a Filipino sour soup in tamarind broth with vegetables.


Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 kilogram pork baby back ribs
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 tomatoes, wedged
  • 1 white radish, sliced
  • 1 bundle long beans, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 bundle water spinach, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 bundle okra, ends trimmed
  • Water
  • Knorr Sinigang sa Sampalok Mix Original, to taste
  • Fish sauce, to taste

Steps

  1. Simmer pork ribs in a pot of water until tender, then strain. Set pork ribs and broth aside separately.
  2. Heat oil in a pot. Sauté the onion and tomato until the onion starts to separate and the tomato softens.
  3. Add pork ribs and continue sautéing until the pork turns light brown in color.
  4. Pour broth and cover the pot. Let the broth boil.
  5. Add Knorr Sinigang Mix and radish. Stir and cover the pot. Continue cooking until the radish is half done.
  6. Add long beans and okra. Cook until the green vegetables are half done.
  7. Season with fish sauce and stir in water spinach. Let simmer for 2 minutes.
  8. Ready to serve.

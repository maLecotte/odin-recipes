Corned Beef Shawarma Wrap

Description

A quick shawarma wrap fusion dish that uses canned corned beef with spices to mimic traditional shawarma wraps.


Ingredients

  • 210 grams canned corned beef
  • 2 teaspoons granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 3 pita wrap
  • 1/2 cucumber, hollowed and sliced
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 6 tablespoons processed cheese, shredded
  • Japanese mayo

Steps

  1. Warm the pita on a pan over medium-high heat for 30-60 seconds until soft or toasted. Set Aside.
  2. Render the corned beef in a pan.
  3. When there's not much liquid fat left, add the sugar, paprika, cumin, and soy sauce and stir until combined. Set Aside.
  4. Put the filling in the middle of the pita, starting with cheese, corned beef, cucumber, tomato, and mayo.
  5. Fold the bottom and sides in. Repeat steps 4 and 5 for the remaining pita wraps.
  6. Ready to serve.

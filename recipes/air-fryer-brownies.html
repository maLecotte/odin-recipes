Air Fryer Brownies

Description

Brownies made quick and easy with an air fryer.


Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 1/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/4 cup chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Stir the flour, cocoa powder, and salt together in a bowl. Set Aside.
  2. In another bowl, stir the melted butter, sugar, and vanilla extract together. Once combined, add the egg and stir again until all combined.
  3. Add the dry mixture to the wet mixture and stir until no streaks of flour remain. Fold in some of the chocolate chips.
  4. Preheat the air fryer to 340°F or 170°C.
  5. Line a parchment paper in the air fryer and pour the batter. Sprinkle the remaining chocolate chips on top.
  6. Bake for 10-14 minutes or until brownies have formed a thin crust and appear set in the middle.
  7. Let cool and ready to serve.
